# 🎮 DatFiles

A collection of high-definition modular DAT overhauls for Final Fantasy XI via **[XIPivot](https://github.com/Darkdoom/XIPivot)**.

---

## 🚀 Quick Install (XIPivot)

1. Drop the pack folder directly into your XIPivot DATs directory:

`	ext
Windower4/addons/XIPivot/data/DATs/
└── Tronmenos/
    └── ROM3/3/82.DAT
`

2. In your Windower console:
`	ext
//pivot add Tronmenos
`

Or add to Windower4/addons/XIPivot/data/settings.xml:
`xml
<overlays>Tronmenos,zones,objects,NextHD</overlays>
`

---

## ⚡ Available Packs

*Zero-glare retro-futuristic cybernetic overhaul for Temenos. Replaces the blinding white floor and stone walls with matte obsidian surfaces, glowing neon cyan energy conduits, and digital circuit freeways.*

* **Target DAT**: ROM3/3/82.DAT
* **Format**: Ultra HD ( \times 2048$) Direct3D DXT1
